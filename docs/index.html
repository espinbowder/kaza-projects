A project can count in several WDAs, so these numbers can't be added up. Whole-KAZA projects count in all six; other projects count where at least 2% of their area lies in the WDA.

Countries · projects per country

How to read these numbers

  • The projects are those reported by the organisations involved. The list is not complete, and the details are as reported.
  • Counts per country, WDA, theme, funder or partner can't be added up: one project often works in several countries or WDAs, covers several themes and has several funders and partners.
  • Budgets are for the whole project, as reported, and may include work outside KAZA. They can't be split by country, WDA or theme, and not every project discloses its budget, so the total is a lower limit.
  • A project counts as active when its end date has not passed, or when no end date is given.
  • The map shows the areas where projects work. Areas are approximate, and overlapping projects show as darker shading.
